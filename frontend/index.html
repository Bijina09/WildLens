About WildLens

WildLens is a web-based AI system designed to help wildlife researchers review camera-trap images more efficiently. It filters blank or irrelevant images first, then identifies supported wildlife species.

The goal is to reduce the time spent manually checking large collections of images and make the results easier to review in one place.
